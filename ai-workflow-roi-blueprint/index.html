Questions

What happens next?

Do I receive an immediate result?

Yes. You receive a preliminary fit score and labor-capacity estimate before entering contact information. The full Blueprint review requires human validation.

Is the capacity estimate guaranteed savings?

No. It is a directional labor-capacity estimate based on your inputs and a conservative recovery assumption. It excludes implementation cost, software cost, revenue impact, and error reduction.

What happens after I request the full review?

We review fit within one business day. Strong-fit workflows receive a priority strategy-review path. Other submissions receive the most useful next step, including a measure-first or redesign-first recommendation.

Do I need to upload credentials or sensitive data?

No. Do not submit credentials or sensitive records. The assessment begins with process, volume, effort, systems, desired outcome, and control boundaries.
