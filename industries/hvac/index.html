FAQ

Common Questions

Can the AI handle after-hours emergency calls?

Yes — that's where it earns its keep. The AI answers at 2 AM, determines whether it's a true emergency (no heat, no cooling, water leak), books the job or dispatches per your escalation rules, and texts your on-call tech with full details.

Does it integrate with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber?

We build integrations around the field service software you already run. Booked jobs, customer details, and call summaries flow into your existing dispatch workflow rather than another inbox you have to check.

What happens during peak season call floods?

The AI answers every call simultaneously — there is no busy signal and no queue. During the first heat wave or cold snap, when call volume triples overnight, every caller gets booked or helped instead of bouncing to a competitor.

Can it quote prices?

It handles pricing the way you tell it to. Most HVAC companies have it share diagnostic or trip fees, explain membership pricing, and leave equipment quotes to a comfort advisor visit it books for you.

Will it sell maintenance memberships?

Yes. The AI offers your maintenance plan at natural moments — after booking a repair, during seasonal tune-up outreach — and enrolls interested customers or books them with your office to finish signup.
