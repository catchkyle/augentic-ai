FAQ

Common Questions

Can it handle storm-season call surges?

Yes. The AI answers unlimited simultaneous calls, so when a hail storm generates 200 calls in a weekend, all 200 get answered, qualified, and booked for inspections — no busy signals while homeowners work down their list of roofers.

Does it qualify insurance restoration jobs?

The AI asks the questions your sales team needs answered: storm date, visible damage, insurance carrier, whether a claim has been filed, and whether other roofers have inspected. Qualified restoration leads get priority booking.

Can it follow up on outstanding estimates?

Automatically. Every open estimate gets a structured follow-up sequence by phone and text — with your company name on the caller ID — until the homeowner signs, declines, or asks for changes. Deals stop dying of silence.

What about canvassing and referral leads?

Leads from door-knocking, referrals, and lead vendors get the same instant treatment: the AI calls and texts within seconds of the lead hitting your CRM, books the inspection, and confirms the day before so your reps stop driving to no-shows.

Do my crews or sales reps have to answer anything?

No. The AI handles the phone entirely and sends your team clean summaries — to SMS, email, Slack, or Microsoft Teams. Reps see qualified, scheduled appointments instead of a call log full of missed numbers.
