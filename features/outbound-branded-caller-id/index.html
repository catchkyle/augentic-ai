FAQ

Common Questions

What is branded caller ID?

Your outbound calls display your registered business name on the recipient's phone instead of an unknown number or 'Spam Likely.' We handle carrier registration and number reputation so your calls arrive looking like what they are — a business the customer knows.

Is AI outbound calling compliant?

We build campaigns around consent and compliance: calls go to your own customers and inbound leads, honor contact preferences and do-not-call requests, operate within permitted calling hours, and the agent identifies itself. This is follow-up automation for people in your pipeline — not robocalling.

What kinds of calls can the AI make?

Lead follow-up within seconds of form submission, appointment confirmations and reminders, estimate and quote chasing, renewal and reactivation outreach, review requests, and no-show rebooking. Anywhere your team should be calling but isn't.

What happens when someone answers?

A natural conversation. The AI holds a real dialogue — reschedules the appointment, answers questions, books the meeting — and transfers to your team live when the conversation warrants it. No answer? It leaves a voicemail and follows up by text.

How much more do branded calls get answered?

Unbranded or flagged numbers routinely see answer rates under 10%. Displaying a recognized business name typically lifts answer rates dramatically — and every answered call is a follow-up conversation that actually happens.
