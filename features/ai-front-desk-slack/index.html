The Problem

An AI that answers calls perfectly still fails if your team never sees what happened.

Another Dashboard Nobody Checks

The Team Finds Out Too Late

A lead calls at 10 AM, and sales hears about it in tomorrow's standup. The entire value of instant answering evaporates when the human follow-up lags by a day.

Escalations With No Route

When something urgent hits the phone lines, “the office will pass it along” isn't a protocol. Without structured routing, critical calls depend on whoever happens to check messages.

FAQ

Common Questions

What shows up in Slack?

Whatever you configure: real-time notifications for every answered call with summary and caller details, transcripts, new-lead alerts, booked and cancelled appointments, and urgent escalations. Routed to the channels you choose.

Can different calls go to different channels?

Yes. Sales calls to #sales, urgent issues to #on-call, new bookings to #operations — routing follows your rules by call type, urgency, location, or department, so every alert reaches the person who should act on it.

Can we act on calls from inside Slack?

Your team sees the full context — who called, what they needed, what the AI did — and can claim follow-ups, discuss the lead in-thread, and jump straight to the caller's record. The handoff from AI to human happens where your team already works.

How hard is setup?

Minutes. We connect the agent to your Slack workspace, you pick the channels and routing rules, and call intelligence starts flowing. No new dashboard for your team to remember to check.

What about urgent calls?

Urgent calls trigger immediate alerts with @-mentions to your designated on-call people, following escalation rules you define — so a 2 AM emergency pings the right phone, not a shared inbox nobody reads until morning.
